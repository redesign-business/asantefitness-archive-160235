# Proof: A Santé Lakeside Fitness

## Facility & Infrastructure

- 9,000 square foot gym facility located on the shores of Lake Tahoe in downtown Tahoe City, California
- Located at 850 North Lake Blvd., Tahoe City, CA — just behind Safeway
- Cardio equipment, free weights, selectorized equipment
- Locker rooms, showers, and a sauna
- Pilates studio
- Group fitness / yoga room equipped with a Medify Air filter and infrared heat

## Services & Programs

- Group fitness classes (all classes free for members)
- Pilates
- Personal training (one-on-one or pairs)
- Daily, weekly, and monthly memberships
- 10-visit punch cards and drop-in day passes
- 30-Day Challenges program: monthly fitness challenges posted online with daily follow-up; can be done at home or at the gym
- Facebook Group Challenge page for community support with challenges
- Active fitness blog with regular updates dating back to 2012
